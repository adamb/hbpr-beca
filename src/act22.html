Invest in the Future of Puerto Rico’s Tech Workforce

The Holberton Scholarship Fund is administered by the Fundación Comunitaria de Puerto Rico (FCPR). Because the fund is housed within the FCPR—a 501(c)(3) and 1101.01(a) certified philanthropic institution—your contributions are processed in full compliance with Puerto Rico tax incentive requirements.

Fulfilling Your Annual Commitment

  • Act 60 Decree Holders: You may allocate $5,000 of your $10,000 annual requirement to the Holberton Scholarship Fund at the FCPR. The remaining $5,000 must be directed to an organization currently on the official CECFL list, which focuses on child poverty and community impact.
  • Act 22 / Other Decrees: You may direct your full annual donation requirement to the Holberton Scholarship Fund at the FCPR.

Information for Your CPA

To ensure your donation is correctly credited toward your decree compliance, please use the following details:

  • Legal Recipient: Puerto Rico Community Foundation, Inc. (FCPR)
  • Fund Name: Holberton Puerto Rico Scholarship Fund
  • EIN: 66-0413230
  • Tax Status: 501(c)(3) / 1101.01(a)(2)

Consult your tax advisor to confirm your specific decree requirements. All donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

FCPR Credentials
