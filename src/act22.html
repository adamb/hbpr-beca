Fulfilling Your Act 60 Requirements

Holberton School Puerto Rico is a certified 1101.01(a) non-profit and a qualifying recipient for the discretionary portion of your annual Act 60 contribution.

  • For Act 60 Decree Holders: You may allocate $5,000 of your total $10,000 annual requirement to Holberton School Puerto Rico. The remaining $5,000 must be directed to an organization currently on the official CECFL list focused on child poverty and community impact.
  • For Act 22 / Other Investors: Your full $5,000 annual contribution can be directed to our scholarship fund.

Note: We recommend consulting your CPA to ensure your allocation meets your specific decree’s requirements. Donations are facilitated through Fundación Comunitaria de Puerto Rico, a registered 501(c)(3) public charity (Tax ID 66-0413230).

Act 60 Holders (formerly Act 22)

We are fully registered in Puerto Rico as well as the United States and on the Hacienda list of approved non-profits. We are an Act 60 (formerly Act 22) approved non-profit organization and all donations can be credited towards your mandatory yearly donation.
